# Marketing Systems Lab

**Building depth in marketing systems, AI fluency and measurement. In public.**

I started in brand, then moved into tech: fintech, marketplace, global SaaS. Craft is where I come from. Systems is where I am going deep.

This lab is where I do it. Every piece answers a business question, and every piece ends with the part a pure analyst often skips: **so what should marketing do differently?**

---

## Three pillars

| Pillar | What lives here | Proof type |
|---|---|---|
| [Measurement & Analytics](measurement-analytics/) | Marketing analytics case studies: KPI trees, funnels, experiments, attribution | Real, simulated or analysed cases, labelled |
| [AI Fluency](ai-fluency/) | Agent skills by role: growth marketer, product marketing, GTM, financial modeling | Skills I adapted and tested, with test notes |
| [Marketing Systems](marketing-systems/) | Workflow automation: tracking, routing, lifecycle | Working workflows, built step by step |

Plus a [Learning Log](learning-log/): what I studied, how I challenged it, and where I applied it.

## Honest labels

| Label | Meaning |
