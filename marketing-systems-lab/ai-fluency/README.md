## Rule
A skill enters this folder only when it has:
1. `SKILL.md` written or adapted in my own words
2. `SOURCE.md` crediting what it was adapted from
3. `tests.md` showing at least 2 runs, where it failed and what I changed

Skills I have read but not tested live in [`library.md`](../library.md).
