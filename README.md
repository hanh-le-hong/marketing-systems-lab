# Marketing Systems Lab

**Building depth in marketing systems, AI fluency and measurement. In public.**

I started in brand, then moved into tech: fintech, marketplace, global SaaS. Craft is where I come from. Systems is where I am going deep. This lab is where I do it.

**Start here:** the [Progress](#progress) table. Each row links to a finished piece once it ships.

---

## The craft lens

Plenty of people can pull a number. Fewer can say what marketing should do because of it.

Every piece in this lab ends with a section called **So what for marketing**: the decision the analysis supports, and the trade-off it creates. That section is where my brand and product marketing background does the work.

## Honest labels

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Real** | From my own work. Numbers shown as ratios or ranges, never internal data |
| **Simulated** | Built on public or synthetic data to practise something I have not done at work |
| **Analysis** | Another company's public case, with my evaluation |

Skills in AI Fluency carry one more mark: **Tested**, meaning I have run them at least twice and logged where they failed.
